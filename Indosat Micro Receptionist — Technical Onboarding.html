PART 1
Key user journeys
1 Owner Onboarding
JOURNEY 1 · OWNER
Owner onboarding
1
Invite
Owner opens a link or scans a QR code.
2
Business profile
Answers become business memory: name, hours, services.
3
Product catalog
Owner uploads a picture of the menu or types in product details.
4
Agent created & phone line attached
Receptionist agent created from the profile; number attached.
CONFIGURABLE
Every step of onboarding is configurable via a template, e.g. what questions are asked and how they are asked.

Micro Receptionist — Technical Onboarding
PART 2
Key user journeys
2 Customer Ordering
JOURNEY 3 · CUSTOMER
Customer ordering
1
Call in
Customer calls the business number; the agent greets with the business name.
2
Build the order
Items, quantities and notes captured as an order list.
3
Read back
The agent repeats the full order and total; the customer confirms.
4
Notify and close
Owner is notified on SMS.
CONFIGURABLE
Agent behaviors and customer information, e.g. agent greetings, customer name.

Customer follow-up
1
Receive order confirmation
AGENT SAYS
“Kavarna confirmed your order. Please pay through [payment link].”
2
Fulfilled order
AGENT SAYS
“Your order is ready for pickup.”
3
Canceled order
AGENT SAYS
“Would you like to finish your order with Kavarna?”
MEMORY
Each order is kept as its own entity with its history; properties of each order, such as status and total price, can be updated.
